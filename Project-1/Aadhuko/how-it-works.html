Two ways to make a difference

Give money, or give
something useful.

Requests on Aadhuko should be specific. Always follow the home’s current wishlist and confirm details first.

₹MONEY SUPPORT

Help cover a shared cost

A contribution can help the home buy the items or services named in its request.

  • School supplies: notebooks, pencils, art materials, or exam fees.
  • Nutritious food: pantry staples or ingredients requested by the home.
  • Learning equipment: a shared computer, printer, or internet access.
  • Play and wellbeing: sports kits, games, or activity materials.

Agree on the amount and purpose with the home before sending money through a channel it provides.

＋ITEM DONATIONS

Offer an item they can use

Useful goods can help when they match a current request and are safe, complete, and in good condition.

  • Electronics: working laptops, tablets, headphones, or charging accessories.
  • Books: storybooks, age-appropriate reference books, and dictionaries.
  • Toys and games: building blocks, puzzles, board games, or art kits.
  • Equipment: sports gear, musical instruments, desks, or storage shelves.

Ask before delivering. Confirm age suitability, condition, transport, and whether the home has space for it.
